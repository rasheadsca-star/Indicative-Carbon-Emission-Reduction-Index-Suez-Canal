Physical CO₂ factors: IMO MEPC.346(78). Example lower heating values: IMO-hosted maritime roadmap.

Not provided: actual marine engine specifications, verified speed/power curves, AIS voyage, official SCNT toll, compliance ETS/FuelEU cost, methane slip, N₂O, WtW emission factors, fuel certificates. Alternative-fuel machinery assumptions are NOT certified. FAME B100 LHV 37.2 MJ/kg and CO₂ factor 2.834; mass-blended B20 LHV 39.76 MJ/kg. See SCA 2024 base rates and IMO LCA guidelines.
