⚗️ WtW / GFI Framework
WtW Factor = GFI (GHG Fuel Intensity) in gCO₂eq/MJ on LHV basis. Values from FuelEU Maritime Annex I (EU 2023/1805) & IMO MEPC.1/Circ.896. TtW = direct combustion CO₂ only (MARPOL Annex VI factors). WtW/GFI includes upstream (production + transport) emissions. CII uses TtW CO₂ per IMO MEPC.354(78); FuelEU compliance uses WtW/GFI. Versioned reference data — not per-run market fetch.

CARBON ECONOMICS — SUEZ ADVANTAGE ANALYSIS
📌 Carbon Price & Suez Canal Advantage
For eligible Asia–Europe trades, the Suez route is generally shorter than routing around the Cape of Good Hope. The resulting fuel, time and emissions advantage is vessel- and voyage-specific. The simulator therefore reports a transparent scenario result rather than a fixed promotional claim.
